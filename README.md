## 🌟 About Me

I am a second-year **Electronics & Communication Engineering undergraduate** at **St. Joseph's College of Engineering, Chennai (2025 – 2029)** with a strong academic foundation (**CGPA: 8.96 in Sem 1 • 8.89 in Sem 2**).

I bridge the gap between physical hardware and high-performance digital software. Whether it is designing circuit schematics and routing PCBs in **KiCad**, flashing firmware onto an **Arduino Uno** or **ARM7 (LPC2129)** microcontroller, or engineering responsive, scalable web applications with **React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Node.js, and Supabase**, I build end-to-end solutions that are practical, fast, and visually refined.

- 🎓 **Undergraduate**: B.E. in Electronics & Communication Engineering at St. Joseph's College of Engineering, OMR, Chennai.
- 🛠️ **Engineering Across The Stack**: Designing interactive frontends, architecting secure backends with relational databases, and integrating embedded sensors, servos, and microcontrollers.
- 🏆 **Proven Innovator**: Two-time collegiate hackathon champion (**MAKE-A-THON 3.0** & **MAKE-A-THON 2.0**).
- 📜 **IIT Kharagpur Certified**: **NPTEL Elite Certificate** in *Introduction to Internet of Things* with a consolidated score of **83%**.
- 💡 **Philosophy**: Ship working prototypes early, uphold clean architectural principles, and craft intuitive, accessible user experiences.
